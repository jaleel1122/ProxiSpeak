# ProxiSpeak

**ProxiSpeak** is a 2D virtual workspace with proximity-based communication, designed to make remote collaboration feel more natural, interactive, and spontaneous.

Instead of relying entirely on traditional meeting rooms and scheduled calls, ProxiSpeak places users inside a shared virtual environment where they are represented by avatars. Users can move freely around the workspace, approach other participants, and communicate based on their proximity to one another.

The goal is to recreate some of the natural interactions of a physical workspace — such as walking over to a teammate, having a quick conversation, joining a nearby discussion, or simply seeing who is around — within a digital environment.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Remote collaboration tools are extremely useful, but communication often becomes highly structured.

Most traditional platforms require users to:

- Create or join meetings.
- Send meeting links.
- Schedule conversations in advance.
- Manually enter and leave calls.
- Switch between multiple communication tools.

This works well for formal meetings, but it often removes the spontaneous interactions that naturally happen in physical offices, classrooms, labs, and collaborative spaces.

For example, in a physical workspace you can simply walk toward a teammate, ask a quick question, discuss something for a few minutes, and walk away when the conversation is finished.

ProxiSpeak aims to bring this kind of interaction into a virtual environment.

---

## 💡 Core Idea

The core idea behind ProxiSpeak is **spatial communication**.

Users exist inside a shared 2D workspace as movable avatars. Their position inside the environment determines how they can interact with other users.

Instead of explicitly joining a voice room every time, users can move closer to other participants to communicate with them.

This creates a workspace where communication can happen more naturally through:

- Spatial presence
- Avatar movement
- User proximity
- Real-time interaction
- Proximity-based communication

The virtual environment therefore becomes more than just a visual interface — it becomes part of how users communicate.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗺️ 2D Virtual Workspace

Users interact inside a shared 2D environment representing a digital workspace.

The workspace provides a visual sense of location and presence, allowing participants to understand where other users are within the environment.

---

### 🎮 Player / Avatar Movement

Each participant is represented by an avatar that can move around the workspace.

Movement allows users to explore the environment, approach teammates, move between areas, and naturally control who they interact with.

The movement system forms one of the fundamental building blocks of the ProxiSpeak experience.

---

### 👥 Real-Time Multiplayer Presence

ProxiSpeak is designed around a shared environment where multiple users can be present simultaneously.

As users move around the workspace, their positions and presence can be synchronized so that participants can see and interact with each other in real time.

This helps create the feeling of occupying the same digital space rather than simply being connected to the same meeting.

---

### 🎙️ Proximity-Based Voice Communication

Voice interaction is based on the distance between users inside the virtual workspace.

When users move closer to one another, communication can become available or more prominent. As they move farther apart, the interaction can decrease or end naturally.

This allows conversations to form dynamically without requiring users to constantly create, join, or leave separate calls.

---

### 🤝 Collaborative Workspace Experience

ProxiSpeak aims to create an environment where remote teams can interact in a more informal and presence-driven way.

Potential use cases include:

- Remote development teams
- Student collaboration
- Virtual classrooms
- Project discussions
- Hackathon teams
- Online communities
- Study groups
- Distributed workplaces

The objective is to combine the accessibility of remote collaboration with some of the social and spatial characteristics of working together physically.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

The intended interaction flow is simple:

### 1. Enter the Workspace

A user joins the shared ProxiSpeak environment and appears inside the workspace as an avatar.

### 2. Explore the Environment

The user can move around the 2D workspace and see other participants who are currently present.

### 3. Approach Other Users

Users can move toward teammates or groups they want to interact with.

### 4. Communicate Through Proximity

When users are within an appropriate proximity, voice communication can become available based on their relative distance.

### 5. Move Between Conversations

Users can move away from one group and approach another, allowing conversations to form and dissolve naturally.

This creates a more fluid communication experience compared with repeatedly joining and leaving traditional meeting rooms.
